## Quick Reference: Key Auction Parameters

- **Total Auction Capacity:** 3,840 MW (Luzon Grid)
- **Lot 1 (Existing Plants):** 1,200 MW – 1,800 MW | 24% min CF | 5-Year Extension | \$600/kW Brownfield AIPC
- **Lot 2 (New-Build Plants):** 2,040 MW – 2,640 MW | 37%–42% CF | 15–20 Year PSA | \$900/kW Greenfield AIPC
- **Fuel Hierarchy:** Indigenous Natural Gas $\rightarrow$ Blended Gas $\rightarrow$ Imported LNG
- **FSRU Terminal Fee Cap:** \$1.50/MMBtu
- **Boil-Off Gas (BOG) Allowance:** 0.05%–0.15%/day
- **CCGT Benchmark Heat Rate:** 6,100 BTU/kWh (LHV)
- **Fuel Savings Sharing:** 80% Consumer / 20% Generator Incentive (Option C)
