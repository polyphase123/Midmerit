## Key Regulatory & Technical Insights

1. **Lot 1 Capacity Tranche Fit:**
   - **Lot 1 Target Allocation under DOE TOR:** **1,200 MW – 1,800 MW** (5-Year Extension Period).
   - **Combined Offered Capacity (Bidder 13 + 14):** **1,500 MW** (Santa Rita 1,000 MW + San Lorenzo 500 MW).
   - **Installed Fleet Capacity:** **1,582 MW** (1,052 MW + 530 MW), reserving 82 MW for auxiliary load, spinning reserve, and operational safety headroom.

2. **Fuel Transition Classification:**
   - Both facilities are officially registered and evaluated under **Fuel Type: Blended** (combining declining Malampaya indigenous supply with imported regasified LNG from FGEN's Batangas FSRU terminal).

3. **GATP & Brownfield AIPC Applicability:**
   - As Lot 1 operational facilities, both Bidders are subject to the **Brownfield AIPC benchmark of .00 / kW** and the minimum **24.0% Net Capacity Factor** operating floor, ensuring non-fuel capital cost discipline.
