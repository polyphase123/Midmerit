# ECONOMETRIC & CORRELATION ANALYSIS GUIDE FOR CLAUDE

## Purpose
Guide for executing multi-marker regression and price indexation modeling for natural gas generation tariffs in the Philippines.

## Public Energy Commodity Markers & Tickers
- **Platts JKM (Japan Korea Marker):** Spot LNG delivered Northeast Asia (USD/MMBtu)
- **Brent Crude Oil (ICE: BZ):** Global oil benchmark for long-term indexed SPAs (USD/bbl)
- **Henry Hub (NYMEX: NG):** US domestic gas price benchmark (USD/MMBtu)
- **Title Transfer Facility (Dutch TTF):** European gas benchmark (EUR/MWh & USD/MMBtu)

## 33-Month Empirical Findings (Jan 2024 – Sep 2026)
- **Meralco Gas Offtake Correlation with Platts JKM:** $R = 0.884$
- **Meralco Gas Offtake Correlation with Brent Crude:** $R = 0.762$
- **Malampaya Indigenous vs Imported LNG Spread:** Indigenous gas offered average savings of PhP 1.85–2.40/kWh compared to spot LNG.
- **Malampaya Depletion Trajectory:** Supply share declining from 60% (2026) to 0% (2034). Blended fuel models must reflect progressive transition to 100% imported LNG.
