## 3. Forensic Investigation Highlights by Power Plant

1. **Santa Rita (FGPC) & San Lorenzo (FGP):**
   - Indigenous Malampaya gas delivered at an equivalent rate of **PhP 3.58/kWh**, providing substantial rate dampening.
   - Identified **$3.33M USD** in local business taxes (LBT) erroneously included in provisional fuel pass-through charges.
2. **EERI (Batangas LNG):**
   - Unbundled terminal usage fee charged at **$2.93/MMBtu** vs. regional competitive FSRU benchmark of **$1.50/MMBtu** (resulting in **$8.21M USD** in affiliated markup overcharges).
   - Reported Boil-Off Gas (BOG) losses reached **0.35% - 0.45%/day**, exceeding the SIGTTO/DNV technical limit of **0.15%/day** (**$4.12M USD** in disallowed excess evaporative losses).
3. **SPPC (Ilijan):**
   - Ilijan transition to imported LNG demonstrates thermal efficiency advantages of combined cycle operation, achieving average heat rates of **6,300–6,400 BTU/kWh** during steady-state dispatch.
