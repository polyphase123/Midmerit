## 1. Mid-Merit Natural Gas Capacity Auction (DC2026-06-0013 / ERC Case No. 2026-015 RM)

- **Target Capacity:** Approximately **3,840 MW** of mid-merit gas-fired capacity across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.
- **Gas Auction Threshold (GAT) Price:**
  - Serves as the **maximum allowable price ceiling / bid cap**. Bids exceeding the GAT Price are automatically disqualified.
  - Determined under the Two-Resolution Architecture:
    - **Resolution 1 (GAIR):** Establishes the DCF Model methodology, FCPT rules, and WESM settlement without hardcoded numbers.
    - **Resolution 2 (Reference Parameters Resolution):** Formally adopts the specific GATP numbers, AIPC benchmarks ($600/kW brownfield vs. $900/kW greenfield), and heat rate ceilings.
- **Base Rate Policy:** Long-term fixed base capacity recovery (up to 25–30 years), while fuel commodity is regulated through monthly indexation to Platts JKM / Brent with Option C savings sharing.
