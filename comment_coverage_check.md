## CLLC / logistics points

16 points from 9 commenters (MERALCO 3, SPPC & EERI 4, Tokyo Gas 2, Prime CoreGen 2, PEI & SEDI 1, Quezon 1, PMCJ 1, NAC 1, White Paper 1). None asks to remove the CLLC from the GAT Price. The closest is NAC Energy ("approved logistics costs should be adjusted or passed through"). That matches the 19 October restructure, which moves the CLLC into the Fuel Cost.

## Points that bear directly on the 19 October restructure (GATP = CRF; O&M and CLLC moved into the FC)

- **White Paper obs. 1** argues the opposite way (put more, including fuel, into the competed GAT Price). The dispositions need to explain the choice.
- **Tokyo Gas (8.6)**: fixed LNG infrastructure charges are still under-recovered at low dispatch.
- **MERALCO 3**: the recorded answer ("CLLC fixed once embedded in the GAP") no longer holds.
- **PMCJ 2**: consumers now carry O&M and logistics variation up to the caps.
- **DOE DC Sec. 4 / 7.2**: the Fuel Cost Pass-Through is defined as gas costs, so O&M in the FC needs DOE concurrence. This is flagged in the draft at Section 17-A.
